# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient in project discussions. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, publication of another person's private information,
and intentionally disruptive behavior are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, and other
contributions that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns through GitHub's private reporting facilities when
available. If no private channel is available, open an issue asking the
maintainer to establish one without including sensitive details.

This is a small, volunteer-maintained project. Disagreement about a technical
decision is welcome when it stays focused on the work and treats participants
with respect.
